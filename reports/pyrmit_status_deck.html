What we built, what it needs to run,
and what is left

A chat assistant for Swedish land use and building permits that answers from each kommun's own plans and from the law, and cites every claim.

The idea

Ask about a place, get an answer from its plans and the law

Can I add a 25 m² guest house on my plot? (the place is pinned on the map)
Verdict: probably yes, without bygglov, if the plan's building-area limit for the plot still allows it 1. A komplementbostadshus up to 30 m² inside a detaljplan needs no bygglov 2, but closer than 4.5 m to the boundary the neighbours must agree 3…

[1] Detaljplan of the plot: plankarta and bestämmelser · [2] PBL 9 kap. 4 § · [3] PBL 9 kap. 34 §

Illustration of the answer format; each [n] opens the cited passage in its original PDF or statute.

What makes it hard

  • 290 kommuner, each with its own website, plans and PDFs, many of them scanned decades ago.
  • The rules sit in plan maps: codes printed on zones of a drawing.
  • The law changes: PBL 9 kap. was renumbered in 2025 (SFS 2025:974); the models remember the old version.
  • Answers must be sourced: an unsupported claim is worse than no answer.

What it does

  • Gives a verdict, explains a rule, proposes projects or develops an idea.
  • Asks back when the place or the project is unclear.

Chosen on a benchmark

Checked, not trusted

  • Coverage against the kommuner's own plan lists
  • A PDF is dropped only on a near-certain rule or by a second judge
